# capstone-301

## Technical notes:
- vite and react with typescript
- materialUI as the component library
- react-router for routing
- faker.js for generating fake data
- mui/x-charts for charting

## Design notes:
- must be mobile-first and responsive for a variety of screen sizes
- must have light and dark modes, set to dark mode by default
- must support multiple forms of user interaction and filtering

## Product description:
- We run a streaming company that competes with Netflix, Amazon Prime, AppleTV, etc.
- We produce our own content (tv shows, movies, podcasts, etc) in house as well as license content from established studios.
- Our users require an interactive, filterable, sortable dashboard that helps them visualize data.
- Our users need to be able find our what genres and types of content are most popular by region so the know what to invest in more and where to not invest. 
- Sometimes, licensing certain content is worth it because we don't have to pay for production, but other times it isn't.
- Sometimes producing our own tv shows and movies is worth it when they are really good but sometimes they are flops and turn out to be extremely expensive. We would like to indentify what type of content to make more of.
